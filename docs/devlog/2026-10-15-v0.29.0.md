# v0.29.0: Notes

*2026-10-15, Paul Carouge*

Phase 5 starts with notes. The calendar comes next, inside Alcove first; CalDAV sync with the phone's own
calendar apps will follow in a later release. Contacts are still not planned.

## Notes in the database, not in the drive

Two designs were on the table: Markdown files in the drive, or records in the database. Files are
portable and visible to other apps, but live sync, search, tags and edits from two devices are all harder
on top of a file store. Records get all of that from PocketBase for free: rules restrict every note to its
owner, and changes reach other devices through the same realtime channel the drive already uses. Export
to Markdown stays easy, since the body is plain Markdown. The decision is written down in ADR 0027.

## Saving as you type, without losing a word

The editor saves 800 ms after you stop typing, one save at a time, and shows its state: saving, saved, or
not saved with a retry button. The review of the first version found the real work was in the edge cases:

- a save that failed while you switched to another note was forgotten, and the text vanished on the next
  reload. Each note now keeps its own saver until everything is on the server, and leaving the page with
  an unsaved note asks first;
- a late realtime echo of an older save could put old text back in the editor (and move the cursor on
  the phone). Events that are not newer than what the editor already has are now ignored;
- on the phone, tapping back twice popped two screens, and a failed save on close was silent. The editor
  now closes once, waits at most five seconds, and asks before dropping unsaved text. It also saves when
  the app goes to the background.

The Markdown preview on the web uses a small renderer written for Alcove that escapes everything first and
only links http(s) addresses. The security audit found one oddity (a private-use character could repeat an
earlier link's markup inside the same note), fixed before release.

## Smaller things

- The app finally has its own icon: the arch from the logo, warm white on sage, adaptive, with a
  monochrome layer for themed icons. The web had still been showing the default Svelte favicon.
- The selection bar moved to the bottom of the screen as a floating bar, closer to the thumb on phones.
- The Android back button used to leave the app from inside the Files and Albums tabs; it now goes back.

## What went wrong

- Typing right after "New note" went into the previous note, because the editor stayed active while the
  new note was being created. It is now read-only for that moment.
- The end-to-end tests and the temporary preview servers both wanted port 8098 for a while. Two agents
  working at once had to take turns.
