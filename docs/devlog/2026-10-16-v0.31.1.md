# v0.31.1: Room to build

*2026-10-16, Paul Carouge*

v0.31.0 was tagged but never published: its Android job was stopped by GitHub three times in a row, each
time in the middle of the Gradle build, with "The runner has received a shutdown signal".

The project's Gradle settings ask for 8 GB of heap and 4 GB of metaspace, sensible on a laptop. The runners
GitHub gives to private repositories have about 7 GB of memory in total. A single universal APK used to fit
by luck; since v0.30.0 the job builds the universal APK and then the three per-processor APKs, and it no
longer did. A retry worked once for v0.30.0, which hid the cause.

The release job now writes user-level Gradle settings (3 GB heap, a 1 GB Kotlin daemon, two workers) before
building; they take precedence over the project's. Rerunning the v0.31.0 tag could not pick this up, since
a tag runs the workflow as it was at that commit, hence this patch release with the same app.
